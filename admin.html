📸 How to update your photos

  1. Add or remove image files from the Photos/Front Photos, Photos/Left Photos, or Photos/Right Photos folders on your computer.
  2. Come back here → arrange the order by dragging rows up/down → type captions.
  3. Click Save & Download photos.json - a file downloads automatically.
  4. Move that file into your THOCB folder (replace the old one).
  5. Redeploy to Netlify by dragging the THOCB folder. Done!

Note: The first photo in each list is your cover photo shown on the main Stay page.
